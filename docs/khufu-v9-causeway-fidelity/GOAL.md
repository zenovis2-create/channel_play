# Goal

Deliver a playable Khufu V9 causeway corridor from Valley Gate to the V8 Temple Hub with a readable basalt path,
limestone enclosure, red-granite portal rhythm, and exact visual-to-collider ownership.

## Observable Done Surface

- A Windows player can travel from the Valley Gate spawn through Covered Causeway into the V8 Temple Hub.
- The two inherited V5 route-floor colliders remain enabled and aligned with the V9 floor art.
- Every V9 wall, pylon, or overhead structural visual has one simplified V9 BoxCollider proxy.
- Every enabled solid collider intersecting the V9 visual envelope is either V9-owned, one of the two inherited
  floors, or paired with enabled visible geometry; orphaned invisible collision is zero.
- The route keeps at least 1.8m horizontal clearance and 2.2m vertical clearance at sampled points.
- V5 Gate 4, V5 PlayMode traversal, V7 entry behavior, V8 root signatures, V9 static/idempotence/mutation gates,
  Windows normal/proxy/error-metric mutation proof, and the V9 performance budget pass on one bound
  scene/build state.
- Runtime captures are newly created, fully decoded, nonblank, and hash-bound to every BuildReport player file.
- The final scoped commit contains only V9-owned code, assets, docs, receipts, the scene delta, and required
  regression receipts.

## False-Done Conditions

- Visual art passes while a player crosses a visible wall, hits an invisible wall, or cannot reach the V8 hub.
- V9 duplicates or disables an inherited V5 floor collider.
- A new collider exists without a paired V9 structural visual, or decorative inlay receives a collider.
- Valley Gate, Covered Causeway, V7 guides, V8 art, gameplay bindings, or package state drift outside the whitelist.
- Captures are black, wall-filled, editor-only, stale, or do not show the participant moving through the corridor.
- A staged artifact differs from the validated worktree, or any BuildReport runtime payload is omitted from binding.
- The implementation is described as finishing the full map or as an exact archaeological reconstruction.
