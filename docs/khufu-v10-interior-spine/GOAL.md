# Khufu V10 Interior Spine Goal

Updated: 2026-07-12
Baseline commit: `a7ba20fd24034a0c5cf115d21d8d955797abe011`

## Intent

Replace the sparse V4 presentation of Khufu's north entrance, ascending passage, and Grand
Gallery with a production-art traversal slice that reads as architecture embedded in dense
masonry. Preserve the accepted V4 geometry contract, V5 objective graph, and V8/V9 art and
performance evidence while making the authentic interior spine inspectable and playable.

## Completion Surface

V10 is complete only when all of the following are observable on one bound revision:

- a removable `Runtime_Khufu_V10_Interior_Spine` scene root exists beside V4 and V5;
- the north entrance, descending/ascending junction, Gallery Foot, Grand Gallery, and Great Step
  read as one coherent route;
- exactly seven corbel courses per side and 27 bench-slot rhythms per side are visible in the
  compressed Grand Gallery, with the Great Step pair recorded separately;
- the Queen branch, royal suite, subterranean route, SP-BV, SP-NFC, and shafts stay within their
  documented ownership and truth class;
- every enabled structural collider in the V10 envelope has a visible owner, and every declared
  structural visual has a matching collision proxy or explicitly inherited floor;
- a `1.8m x 2.2m` gameplay-clearance route reaches the Great Step boundary and returns through a
  separately tagged HYBRID service route without a permanent one-way trap;
- static, idempotence, mutation, legacy regression, Windows traversal, image, performance, staged
  index, and post-commit validation all pass;
- external Fable and Local Fable receipts report the implementation decision and residual risk.

## False-Done Conditions

The slice is not done if any of these are true:

1. It is a prettier cutaway that cannot be traversed with collision enabled.
2. The gallery resembles a wide staircase but lacks the seven-course corbel, benches, slots, and
   narrow ceiling relationship.
3. Extra invisible colliders exist inside the V10 visual envelope.
4. A successful traversal cannot be made to fail by an independent negative control.
5. The service return is presented as an exact reconstruction of the Well Shaft.
6. V10 claims ownership of the Queen's Chamber, King's Chamber, relieving chambers,
   Subterranean Chamber, SP-BV, or SP-NFC.
7. A screenshot, compile, or Editor-only run is used as proof for a different completion surface.
8. Passing evidence is stale, unbound, or differs from the staged bytes.
9. Gallery route samples do not meet the deterministic enclosure-ray coverage threshold.
