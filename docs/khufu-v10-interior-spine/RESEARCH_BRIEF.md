# Khufu V10 Interior Spine Research Brief

## Method

The baseline combines project research, NotebookLM synthesis, and direct source verification.
NotebookLM deep research found 56 candidates, including sensational secondary claims. Only the two
Harvard Digital Giza records for Maragioglio and Rinaldi were imported from that set. Numeric and
truth claims below are restricted to primary surveys, scholarly architectural analysis,
peer-reviewed measurement papers, and the Egyptian Ministry record.

## Accepted Geometry

- The north entrance is east of the face centerline and leads to a narrow descending passage.
- The ascending passage begins in the descending-passage ceiling, keeps an approximately 26 degree
  incline, and is blocked at its lower end by three granite plugs.
- The Grand Gallery continues the ascent. Survey synthesis gives a lower width near `2.09m`, height
  above `8.60m`, a central path near `1.04m`, side benches near one cubit, and seven major corbel
  steps on the east and west sides.
- Each bench has 27 measured slot positions; the Great Step adds one position per side.
- The Gallery Foot also exposes the low horizontal branch toward the Queen's Chamber.
- The upper west bench contains the service-shaft mouth. The measured shaft is narrow, irregular,
  and unsuitable for ordinary standing traversal.
- The Great Step marks V10's upper ownership boundary before the royal suite.

## Compression And Gameplay Adaptation

V4 compresses the real pyramid to a `56m` base and `35.636m` height. V10 preserves its accepted
marker positions instead of claiming a 1:1 reconstruction. Passage clearances, approach, route
inlay, and the two-way service return are HYBRID adaptations. The service return references the
documented Well Shaft relationship but does not reproduce its measured section or irregular path.

## Segment Classification

The machine-readable contract is `segment-classification.json`. Ratios are approximate design
ratios, not new archaeological measurements.

| Segment | Factual claim retained | Adaptation and approximate ratio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North approach | North-face entrance relationship | Entire path is HYBRID; no historical exterior stair/bridge claim. |
| Entrance to Branch | Entrance-to-descending/ascending junction topology | V4 run is about `0.225x` the measured entrance-to-junction run; clear width/height are about `2.26x/2.11x` the rock corridor section. |
| Branch to Gallery Foot | Ascending-passage relationship and granite-plug boundary | V4 run is about `0.313x` the measured `39.28m`; angle and clearance are HYBRID. |
| Grand Gallery | Ascending relationship, benches, slots, grooves, seven east/west corbels | V4 run is about `0.280x` the surveyed length; target clear width is about `1.63x` while target height is about `0.56x` the surveyed envelope. |
| Queen branch threshold | Low horizontal branch exists at Gallery Foot | Only a boundary portal is shown; scale and termination are HYBRID. |
| Great Step boundary | Great Step precedes the royal suite | Dimensions and visible gate treatment are HYBRID; royal rooms are not V10-owned. |
| Historic service mouth | A narrow service aperture exists at the gallery's west side | Boundary-only, non-traversable, and spatially separated from the adapted route. |
| HYBRID service return | No factual geometry claim | Entire route, clearance, material, and two-way topology are gameplay adaptation. |

The runtime vocabulary deliberately avoids `Well Shaft`. This phrase appears in research prose only.

## Exclusions

- No traversal or room is created for SP-BV or SP-NFC.
- No Queen's Chamber shaft is widened into a player route.
- No purpose is assigned to gallery slots, niches, grooves, or the Well Shaft.
- V10 does not rebuild the Queen's Chamber, antechamber, King's Chamber, relieving system,
  descending bedrock route, or Subterranean Chamber.
