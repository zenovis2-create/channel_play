# Khufu V11 Royal Chamber Circuit

V11 opens the V10 Great Step ownership boundary and adds a production blockout of the upper royal
suite: the short entry, antechamber, King's Chamber, sarcophagus, and a non-traversable cutaway of
the stacked chambers above it.

This slice is an archaeological-gameplay hybrid. The documented room order, dominant materials,
and recognizable architectural features are retained. Clearances, compression, route lighting,
and the cutaway presentation are gameplay adaptations and must not be described as a measured
one-to-one reconstruction.

The execution contract is split across:

- `GOAL.md`: completion surface and false-completion conditions.
- `PLAN.md`: ownership, phases, and rollback boundary.
- `RULES.md`: truth, code, art, and evidence constraints.
- `TEST_PLAN.md`: executable gates and required visual evidence.
- `STATUS.md`: fact, assumption, risk, decision, and progress ledger.
- `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`: accepted source synthesis and exclusions.
- `segment-classification.json`: machine-readable truth labels.
