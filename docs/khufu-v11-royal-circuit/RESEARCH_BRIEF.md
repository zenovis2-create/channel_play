# Khufu V11 Royal Chamber Circuit Research Brief

## Method

The V11 design uses the accepted V10 source set and rechecks the upper-suite claims against the
Egyptian Ministry record, Harvard Digital Giza's Maragioglio and Rinaldi survey, Petrie's measured
survey, and current peer-reviewed architectural diagrams. Speculative energy, hydraulic, acoustic,
and ritual-purpose claims are excluded.

## Accepted Structure

- The King's Chamber is the upper masonry chamber reached from the Grand Gallery through a short
  passage and antechamber.
- The room is lined in red granite, is longer east-west than north-south, and contains a granite
  sarcophagus.
- The antechamber contains granite architecture associated with three portcullis positions and low
  entry/exit passages.
- Five stacked spaces exist above the chamber. The lower roofs use flat granite beams and the upper
  space has a gabled roof. V11 presents this stack as a non-traversable section display.
- Narrow shafts open from the north and south chamber walls. V11 retains only boundary recesses and
  makes no ventilation, stellar, or ritual-purpose claim.

## Compression And Gameplay Adaptation

The project pyramid is already compressed to a `56m` base and uses the V10 route markers. V11 keeps
the documented sequence and dominant proportions while widening passages for a standing player.
The amber route inlay, selectively raised portcullis presentation, observation cutaway, and lighting
are HYBRID presentation choices.

## Exclusions

- No one-to-one survey reconstruction claim.
- No public traversal through the stacked chambers or shaft mouths.
- No unsupported purpose for the antechamber, shafts, sarcophagus, or stacked spaces.
- No Queen's Chamber, Subterranean Chamber, or scan-anomaly geometry.
