# Khufu V12 Queen Circuit Research Brief

## Accepted Facts

- A horizontal passage branches from the lower end of the Grand Gallery and reaches the
  conventionally named Queen's Chamber.
- The chamber is built in limestone and has a pointed/gabled roof.
- A corbelled niche is present in the east wall.
- Two narrow mouths begin in the chamber walls. V12 treats them only as sealed boundary recesses.

The name “Queen's Chamber” is conventional. V12 makes no claim that a queen was buried there and
assigns no purpose to the chamber, niche, or narrow mouths.

## Adaptation

The accepted project pyramid is already compressed to a 56 m base. V12 preserves the existing V10
Gallery Foot and V4 Queen marker, widens only player clearance, and uses a restrained route inlay
for navigation. The inlay, inspection accents, and capture cutaway are presentation devices.
