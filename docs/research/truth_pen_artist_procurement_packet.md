Prepared: 2026-07-26

Status: shortlist ready; nobody contacted, selected, or hired

## Recommended Sourcing Decision

Invite the ranked prospects below to a written pre-production interview. Ask
for a proposal only—no sketch, art test, source file, or generated image.
Prefer a fixed-price contract with reviewable milestones and a project-specific
rights rider. Upwork is the preferred transaction route if the chosen artist
can use it: fixed-price milestones use escrow, and its optional service terms
address work-product assignment and disclosure of background/third-party
materials. Those defaults still require project-owner and jurisdiction review.

Fiverr is a backup, not the default. Its current terms generally allocate
delivered IP after payment, but Gig and Custom Offer exceptions and the separate
commercial-use license require order-specific review. Direct contracting is
acceptable only with a signed project agreement and an owner-approved payment
process.

## Mandatory Eligibility Gates

Reject or pause any response that does not confirm all of the following:

- legal/professional identity and every natural-person contributor;
- no generative AI, tracing, image conditioning, unlicensed likeness, copied
  franchise styling, or undisclosed third-party material;
- a written inventory of tools, references, prior materials, and subcontractors;
- willingness to grant the project commercial use, modification, redistribution,
  marketing, and downstream 3D/Unity rights;
- willingness to sign the final agreement before producing any artwork; and
- delivery of editable source plus the production sheet specified in the RFP.

## Proposal Scorecard

pass/fail and cannot be offset by points.

Advance only a mandatory-gate `PASS` with at least `80/100`. Record the
