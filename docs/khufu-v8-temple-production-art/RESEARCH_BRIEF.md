## Supported Facts

- Khufu's pyramid temple stood on the east side of the Great Pyramid and was connected to the valley complex by a causeway.
- The surviving plan is approximately 52.2m north-south by 40m east-west; the superstructure is mostly lost.
- AERA/ARCE conservation reporting supports a black basalt threshold and irregular basalt court paving,
  limestone wall construction, 50 square red-granite pillars, painted relief fragments, and a narrow northwest corridor.
- The polished white pyramid mass behind the temple would have been the dominant macro landmark.

## Uncertainty Boundary

- The exact intact roof, sanctuary elevation, decorative program, and complete wall arrangement cannot be claimed as known.
- The source FBX's trap corridor, burial chamber, deep interior maze, review markers, and small pyramid cap are useful
  game-production experiments but are not acceptable as a literal reconstruction of Khufu's pyramid temple.
- V8 therefore uses the FBX as a donor kit, not as archaeological proof.
