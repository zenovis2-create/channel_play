# Goal

Deliver a performant Khufu V8 Temple Hub art slice that replaces the visible V5/V6 hub graybox with a
recognizable causeway threshold, basalt court, limestone frame, red-granite rhythm, and painted relief
accents derived from the existing full-environment FBX.

## Observable Done Surface

- The final Windows player shows the causeway-to-court arrival and a readable route into the existing map.
- V8 uses deterministic combined mesh assets with at most 12 renderers and no new colliders, lights, or cameras.
- Existing V5/V6 hub graybox renderers are hidden while their gameplay colliders and bindings remain intact.
- V5 Gate 4, the 1,758.6m CharacterController traversal, V7 entry gates, V8 static gates, Windows build,
  rendered normal/mutation evidence, and performance validation pass on one bound scene/build state.
- Fable returns a final-review ship decision and staged-scope validation rejects unrelated files.

## False-Done Conditions

- The 2,565-renderer FBX is placed directly in the scene or the visible-renderer budget regresses.
- A receipt passes while the player screenshot is black, wall-filled, visually clipped, or still dominated by graybox geometry.
- New art blocks the route, adds gameplay colliders, changes V5-V7 owned source, or alters package state.
- Fictional trap, burial-maze, or pyramid-cap geometry is described as archaeologically verified Khufu temple architecture.
- Evidence refers to stale source, scene, generated-mesh, executable, or screenshot hashes.
