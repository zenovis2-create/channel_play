# Khufu V13 Subterranean Threshold Research Brief

## Accepted Facts

- The Great Pyramid has a descending passage that continues below the pyramid's base.
- A horizontal passage connects the lower descending route to the conventionally named
  Subterranean Chamber.
- The chamber and its pit are unfinished bedrock works. Their intended purpose is not established.

V13 preserves only these relationships. It does not claim survey accuracy, completion state,
ritual meaning, burial use, or a connection to an undiscovered route.

## Project Adaptation

The accepted project pyramid has a compressed 56 m base, while the V10 and V4 branch markers differ
by 2.751 m. V13 connects from the accepted V10 branch and uses project-scale clearance. The
chamber is recentered from the V4 observation `(1, -3.6, 1.5)` to fit the inherited central bedrock
slot; the V4 marker itself does not move. Route inlay, inspection accents, and capture cutaways are
presentation devices, not archaeological evidence.

## Inherited Constraints

- The large V4 foundation colliders remain enabled: the central usable slot is bounded near
  `x=-3`, `x=3`, and `z=8`.
- `V4_Light_Subterranean` remains enabled and must be disclosed in every affected capture.
- The unfinished-pit visual must not create a traversable hole or imply a destination.
