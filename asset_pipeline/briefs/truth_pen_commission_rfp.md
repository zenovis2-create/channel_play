# RFP: Truth Pen Stylized Prop Concept

Status: draft for owner approval; send only after procurement `PASS`; do not
begin art

## Project

Channel Play needs one original, human-created concept sheet for a compact
truth-detection pen used as a shop icon, world pickup, held prop, and OBS-visible
gameplay item. The final 3D asset will target Unity, a `0.22m` handheld scale,
and no more than `3,000` triangles.

## Visual and Production Requirements

- chunky gold-and-charcoal body with a cyan emissive nib;
- strong asymmetric clip and collider-friendly, low-poly silhouette;
- no text, logo, watermark, copied franchise styling, or recognizable person;
- separate body, clip, and emissive-nib material callouts;
- readable at small inventory-icon size and on a broadcast program feed; and
- original design based only on the written project brief and artist-created,
  documented exploration.

## Deliverables

1. Three distinct silhouette thumbnails.
2. One selected direction with two revision rounds.
3. Final three-quarter presentation plus front, side, top, and back orthographic
   views; dimensions, scale reference, material/color callouts, and part breaks.
4. Editable layered source file and lossless flattened exports.
5. Creator/contributor declaration and complete input/reference/tool inventory.

Portfolio links are sufficient for the proposal. Do not produce a sketch, art
test, or any deliverable before the signed agreement and written Gate A `PASS`.
The project must also record a `PASS` from
`python tools/channelctl asset procurement-check truth_pen` before sending this
RFP.

## Proposal Response

Provide:

- legal/professional name, country, contracting entity, and all contributors;
- fixed quote, currency, availability, delivery schedule, and revision terms;
- existing portfolio links showing prop sheets or 3D production handoffs;
- software/tools and intended reference-gathering process;
- confirmation that no generative AI, image conditioning, tracing, or
  undisclosed third-party material will be used;
- any pre-existing/background material proposed for use, or `NONE`; and
- written acceptance of the rights requirements below, subject to the final
  signed contract.

Do not include private tax, banking, identity-document, or payment data in the
proposal.

## Contract and Rights Requirements

The final agreement must identify all parties and jurisdiction and cover,
subject to applicable law:

- exclusive project rights for commercial use, modification, redistribution,
  marketing, and downstream 3D/Unity asset creation;
- editable source files and the right to create, sublicense, and distribute
  derivatives with the game and promotional material;
- disclosure and prior written approval of every background or third-party
  element, reference, subcontractor, and contributor;
- creator warranty that the work is original and the disclosed inputs are
  cleared;
- no generative AI, tracing, image conditioning, unlicensed likeness, logo,
  watermark, or copied franchise styling;
- confidentiality, delivery acceptance, revision, cancellation, credit, and
  portfolio-display terms; and
- further signatures reasonably needed to document the rights chain.

Platform defaults do not replace the project-specific rights review. This RFP
is not itself a contract or legal advice.
